FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful starting points for international entrepreneurs.

Can a non-resident explore company formation in the USA?

Some US business structures and states allow non-residents to establish companies. Requirements, tax obligations and eligibility depend on the circumstances.

Can a non-resident register a UK company?

Non-residents may be able to register a UK company, subject to applicable legal, identity verification and compliance requirements.

How much does company formation cost?

Costs vary by jurisdiction, government fees, service provider and additional services. Request a current itemized quotation before proceeding.

How long does registration take?

Processing times depend on the jurisdiction, application accuracy and the relevant authority. No fixed timeline is guaranteed.

Can I ask about a service before proceeding?

Yes. Use the contact section below to describe your requirements and ask about service availability, pricing and documentation.
